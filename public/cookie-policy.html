Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026.

Essential cookies only. Ace ID uses cookies necessary for authentication, sessions, browser security, passkey challenges and identity-provider flows. The public website does not use advertising cookies.

Essential authentication cookies

Authentication and security cookies keep sign-in state, browser/device controls and temporary identity flows associated with the correct browser.

Third-party authentication

When a user chooses an external sign-in provider, temporary state cookies may be used to protect and complete that authentication flow.

Blocking cookies

Blocking essential cookies can prevent sign-in, passkeys, sessions and OpenID Connect interactions from working correctly.
